## Presentation Format

Your 10-minute presentation should include:

1. **Introduction** (2 min): What is your project? What question or creative goal does it address?
2. **Methods** (3 min): What techniques, tools, and models did you use? Show key code or architecture.
3. **Demo / Results** (3 min): Live demo or play audio/video examples. This is the most important part — lead with sound.
4. **Reflection** (2 min): What worked? What didn't? What did you learn? What would you do differently?

## Presentation Tips

- **Lead with sound**: play your music/audio early — do not make people wait through 8 minutes of slides
- **Keep slides minimal**: code screenshots are fine, but do not read code line by line
- **Prepare audio playback**: test your audio setup BEFORE your slot (speakers, volume, file formats)
- **Have a backup**: if live demo might fail, have pre-recorded video/audio ready
- **Practice timing**: 10 minutes goes fast — rehearse at least once

## Q&A Guidelines

Audience members should ask about:
- Creative choices: why did you choose this approach?
- Technical details: how does the model/system work?
- Evaluation: how do you know it's working well?
- Extensions: what would you do with more time?

## Submission (Due Day of Presentation)
