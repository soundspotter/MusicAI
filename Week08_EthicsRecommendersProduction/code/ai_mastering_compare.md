# AI Mastering Tools — Comparison Guide

## What is Mastering?

Mastering is the final step in music production: preparing a mix for distribution. It involves:
- **EQ**: Balancing frequency content
- **Compression**: Controlling dynamic range
- **Limiting**: Maximizing loudness without clipping
- **Stereo imaging**: Adjusting width and balance
- **Format conversion**: Preparing for streaming, vinyl, CD

## AI Mastering Services

### LANDR (landr.com)
- **How it works**: Upload a WAV/MP3, AI analyzes and masters automatically
- **Strengths**: Fast, consistent, affordable ($4-9/track or subscription)
- **Weaknesses**: Limited control, can over-compress, one-size-fits-all
- **Best for**: Demos, independent releases, quick turnaround

### iZotope Ozone (izotope.com)
- **How it works**: AI-powered Master Assistant suggests settings, user can tweak
- **Strengths**: Professional quality, full manual control, AI as starting point
- **Weaknesses**: Expensive ($199+), requires DAW knowledge
- **Best for**: Serious producers who want AI assistance, not replacement

### Dolby.io Mastering (dolby.io)
- **How it works**: Cloud API for automated mastering
- **Strengths**: Scalable, API-driven, good for batch processing
- **Weaknesses**: Less creative control than iZotope
- **Best for**: Platform integration, automated workflows

### CloudBounce (cloudbounce.com)
- **How it works**: Upload and auto-master with genre-specific presets
- **Strengths**: Genre-aware, multiple output formats
- **Weaknesses**: Limited fine-tuning
- **Best for**: Quick genre-appropriate mastering

## Class Exercise

1. Take a raw mix (unmixed or roughly mixed audio file)
2. Master it with LANDR (free trial available)
3. Compare with the unmastered version
4. Discuss: What did the AI do well? What would a human engineer do differently?

## Discussion Questions

2. Is "good enough" mastering acceptable for most music?
