# Activity 5a: AI vs. Human Listening Critique

**Week 5 — Thursday, in class (~30 minutes)**
**Format**: pairs, then full-class debrief

---

## Overview

You will hear 6 short audio excerpts. Three were generated by an AI music transformer; three were composed and performed by human musicians. All excerpts are from the same or closely related genres and styles — the comparison is fair. Your job is to evaluate each excerpt on its musical merits and then make your best guess about which is AI-generated and which is human.

This activity also doubles as preparation for the **Midterm Critique** assignment.

---

## Instructions

Listen to each excerpt (approximately 30–45 seconds each). With your partner, rate each excerpt on the four criteria below, then record your AI/Human guess.

**Criteria definitions:**

- **Coherence** (1–5): Does it hang together as a piece? Does each moment follow logically from the one before? Is there a sense of musical continuity? *(1 = incoherent, falls apart; 5 = highly unified)*
- **Surprise** (1–5): Does it do something unexpected? Does it subvert your expectations in a musically interesting way — not just randomness, but meaningful deviation? *(1 = utterly predictable; 5 = genuinely surprising)*
- **Development** (1–5): Does it go somewhere? Does the excerpt build, change, or evolve over its duration? *(1 = static, no development; 5 = clear purposeful development)*
- **Character** (1–5): Does it have a distinctive personality or voice — something that makes it recognizable and memorable? *(1 = generic, forgettable; 5 = strong distinctive character)*

## Debrief Questions (full class)

After the reveal:

1. How many did you correctly identify as AI vs. human? Were there patterns in your errors?
2. What specific musical cues did you use to make your guesses? List them.
3. Were the AI excerpts rated higher or lower than the human excerpts on any particular criterion?
4. What does the error rate in this room reveal about the current state of AI music generation?
5. Did rating on the four criteria help you make more accurate guesses, or was it a separate task?

---

## Connection to Midterm Critique

The Midterm Critique assignment (due Week 5) asks you to evaluate a set of AI-generated vs. human-composed pieces in writing, using these same four criteria plus your own additional dimensions. Your notes from this activity are a starting point. For the assignment, you will also be asked to explain your reasoning in prose, not just scores.

---

## Instructor Notes

**Selecting excerpts**: choose 3 AI-generated + 3 human pieces that are closely genre-matched. Good pairings:
- Baroque-style keyboard (Music Transformer generation vs. a short Bach prelude excerpt)
- Jazz piano (transformer generation vs. a less-famous Bill Evans or Ahmad Jamal excerpt)
- Contemporary classical piano (AMT output vs. a Satie or Feldman excerpt)

Avoid very famous pieces — recognition would confound the task. Avoid very long excerpts — 30–45 seconds is enough for the criteria to register.

**Sequencing**: randomize AI/Human order; do not alternate predictably. Consider playing excerpts twice — once for initial impression, once while rating.

**Reveal format**: announce which were AI after all 6 are rated; tally guesses on the board before revealing; this makes the accuracy visible.
