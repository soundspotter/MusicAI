## Discussion Questions

1. How does transformer-generated music differ from Markov-generated music? What structural properties change?
2. What does "long-term structure" mean in music? At what timescales do we perceive musical form, and can transformers capture those timescales?
3. Can transformers learn musical form (e.g., ABA, sonata-allegro)? What evidence would convince you?
4. The Anticipatory Music Transformer uses an interleaved tokenization that separates melody from accompaniment. How does this design choice affect what the model can learn?
5. Temperature controls the randomness of generation. What is the musical analog of "temperature"? When do performers choose to be more or less predictable?
